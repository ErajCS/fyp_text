My tomato leaves are turning yellow and curling at the edges. What could be the problem?
This is often a sign of "Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus" or a nutrient deficiency like Magnesium. Check the undersides of the leaves for whiteflies. If no pests are found, try applying Epsom salts to the soil.
What is the best time to water my wheat crop during the summer season?
It is best to water wheat early in the morning or late in the evening to minimize evaporation. Critical watering stages are crown root initiation (20-25 days after sowing) and the flowering stage.
