1. Preamble: The PQNK Lens 
The Paedar Qudratti Nizam Kashatqari (PQNK) is not merely a set of agricultural practices; 
it is a paradigm shift. It is a philosophy that views the farm as a self-regenerating, pristine 
ecosystem. The PQNK lens moves us away from the extractive and input-intensive model of 
agriculture, which has depleted soils, depleted water tables, and depleted farmer prosperity. 
Instead, PQNK works in harmony with nature's intelligence, fostering soil biology to unlock 
unparalleled productivity, sustainability, and profitability. This paper applies the PQNK lens 
to wheat production, revealing a pathway to national prosperity. 
2. The Problem: The Unsustainable Conventional Wheat Cycle 
Conventional wheat farming is trapped in a cycle of high costs and diminishing returns. 
•
High Input Costs: Farmers are burdened by the costs of repeated tillage, synthetic 
fertilizers (DAP, Urea), weedicides, and frequent irrigation. 
•
Stagnant Yields: The average yield often plateaus around 30 maunds per acre, 
unable to compensate for rising input costs. 

•
Soil Degradation: Chemical fertilizers and tillage destroy soil organic matter and 
beneficial microbial life, leading to long-term fertility loss. 
•
Low Profit Margins: The combination of high costs and a commodity-market price 
keeps farmer incomes low and vulnerable. 
3. The PQNK Solution: A Symphony of Regenerative Practices 
PQNK for wheat is a precise, knowledge-based system that dramatically reduces external 
inputs while boosting yield and quality.

not a subsidized burden. 
Comparative Impact:

a premium, value-added product. It could potentially rival or surpass forex earnings from 
certain commodity sectors that have far higher production and export complexities. 
Conclusion of the Scenario: 
A 20% adoption of PQNK presents a low-risk, high-reward pathway to generating 
substantial forex reserves. It builds a powerful case for prioritizing agri-exports as a key 
pillar of national economic strategy, starting with this readily achievable target. 
Conclusion: The PQNK system increases net profit per acre from PKR 66,000 to PKR 
332,500. This is an increase of over 400%. The farmer's income is not just improved; it is 
revolutionised.

2. Export Boom: Pakistan becomes a global powerhouse for high-value "Organic Plus"

3. Environmental Resilience:

• Soil Revived: Millions of acres of farmland begin to regenerate.

fertilizers and pesticides. 
4.
Food Security: With a 50% increase in yield per acre on half the area, total national 
wheat production would see a significant net increase, enhancing food 
security.
