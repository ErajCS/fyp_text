Ending Myths – Achievements at a Glance
- Industrial Agriculture Misled
Developed processes and machinery for direct seeding without plowing.
PQNK requires minimal labor: sow directly, harvest directly, and minimal tillage in between.
Enabled sowing counted seeds at precise spacing to save cost and increase efficiency, resulting in larger crop plants and healthy yields.
- Replacing Toxic Inputs with Soil Regeneration
Practically demonstrated in farmers’ fields that nutrient-rich crops can be grown without poison.
Crops can be grown without fertilizers and pesticides, resulting in high-quality, nutrient-rich food that improves consumer health and reduces food intake.
Low glycemic index food reduces hunger.
Besides low-cost healthy organisms, this food also reduces the need for feed, milk, meat, and raw material production.
- Reduced Irrigation
Reduced irrigation by up to 100%, relying entirely on rain, dew, and moisture, and in most other cases up to 90%.
With Soil Moisture Management (SMM), PQNK is a solution to water scarcity and eliminates the need for big data collection with drones.
Keeping plants near wilting point forms a dense mineral-water solution for uptake, making them healthy and nutritious to eat.
- PQNK: Regeneration, Sustainability, Autonomy
It was believed that urban life is better for us, but in the long run, the opposite proved true.
PQNK farming is a skilled activity that will create jobs, ensuring youth retention in rural areas, and economic independence for farmers, resulting in a dignified livelihood, food security, autonomy, thus reducing famine, malnutrition, and preventing many diseases.
PQNK can transform Pakistan’s economy from food and grain importer to surplus food exporter, bringing prosperity not only to farmers but to all Pakistanis.
- Weeds Are the Solution
PQNK unlocked the algorithm of nature and helped us see that weeds are friends, not enemies, and essential for good soil health.
PQNK can reduce floods, droughts, pests, cold, strong winds, and other climate challenges.
- Continuous Production Cycle
PQNK makes it possible to produce fresh food year-round.
This continuous supply is based on multi/circular production and provides the highest quality with minimal input.
- PQNK: A Solution for Collective Well-being
PQNK is not just a solution for agriculture, welfare, and health.
By reversing soil degradation, water reservoirs are replenished, moisture in the air is reduced, and ocean degradation is reversed.

Interventions and Achievements in Chronological Order
1973–1977
- Imported farming equipment and adapted it to local conditions.
- Converted small plots to long strips for mechanized irrigation.
- Introduced maize, sorghum, sunflower, and potato seeds; promoted spring maize and crop rotation.
- Delta Pine-15 cotton variety increased yield and fiber quality.
- Punjab Government and USAID established On-Farm Water Management (OFWM) project.
1978–1983
- Adopted sugarcane planting techniques from Australia; promoted by Baba Farid Sugar Mill.
- Established IMT tractors and farm machinery dealership in Sahiwal.
- Set up modern farm machinery production facilities in Sahiwal, Mian Channu, Okara, and Depalpur.
- Established Ford tractor dealership in Jhang.
- Established Massey Ferguson and Fiat dealerships in Bahawalnagar and Okara.
- Transferred operational headquarters to Lahore.
- Acquired national distribution for New Holland harvesting machinery; dominated market share.
1984–1987
- Partnership with Cargill led to seed company and citrus juice concentrate plant.
- Introduced laser leveling equipment via Spectra Physics; subsidized by Punjab Government.
- Introduced peanut harvesting technology via Long Manufacturing USA; faced challenges due to poor soil organic matter.
- Acquired national distribution for Toft sugarcane harvesters from Australia; later managed by sugar mills.
1987–1991
- Introduced metal silos for grain storage via Butler Manufacturing Inc. USA.
- Introduced wide-span buildings for industrial use.
- Introduced mechanical cotton pickers via Case International Harvester; faced adoption challenges.
- Acquired Ford tractor manufacturing license; successful local production.
- Established Pioneer Seed Company in Pakistan; boosted maize production and supported poultry and dairy industries.
1992–1996
- Consolidated business operations in Sahiwal, Jhang, Okara, and Bahawalnagar.
- Sold Ford Agricultural Division; global assembly discontinued.
- Acquired Ursus tractor manufacturing license from Poland.
- Supported government in liberalizing farm commodity prices and linking them to international markets.
- Cotton prices rose sharply; farming became profitable; land values increased.
1995–2002
- Discovered Bt cotton seeds resistant to bollworm during a visit to El Paso, USA.
- Imported and tested seeds in Pakpattan; successful results led to widespread adoption.
- Investigated Bt technology and its microbial basis.
- Bt cotton transformed the cotton landscape and supported the textile economy.
- Later, pink bollworm developed resistance, leading to crop losses.
- PQNK emerged as a savior during this crisis.
- Became national distributor for Kenwood Japan; introduced car audio systems.
- Established assembly plant for air conditioners and home appliances under Kentex brand.
