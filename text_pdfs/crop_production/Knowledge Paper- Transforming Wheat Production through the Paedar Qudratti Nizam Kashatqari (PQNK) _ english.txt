1 Introduction: PQNK’s Theory
.
Proactive Natural System Farming (PQNK) is not just a set of agricultural practices, but a new intellectual revolution.
It is a philosophical thought that considers the farm as a self-regenerating, purely natural system.
PQNK’s theory takes us out of this exploitative and input-based agricultural model
that has destroyed the soil’s fertility, groundwater, and farmer’s well-being.
In contrast, PQNK’s intelligence By combining with PQNK, it stimulates soil biology, achieving unprecedented productivity, sustainability and profitability.
This paper sheds light on wheat production from the perspective of PQNK and presents a clear path to national prosperity.
2. Problem: Unsustainable cycle of traditional wheat cultivation
Traditional wheat cultivation is trapped in a cycle of increasing costs and decreasing profits.
High costs Farmers are burdened by repeated tillage, artificial fertilizers (DAP, urea), herbicides, and frequent irrigation.
Stagnation in production: Average production has stagnated at about 30 maunds per acre, which is not able to keep up with the increasing costs.
Soil degradation: Chemical fertilizers and tillage kill soil organisms, reducing the long-term fertility of the soil.
Low profitability: High costs and low market prices have weakened and destabilized the farmer’s income.
