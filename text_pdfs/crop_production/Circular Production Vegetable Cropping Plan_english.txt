PQNK: Cultivating Sustainable Tomorrow Through Regenerative Agriculture Circular Production Vegetable Cropping Plan Circular Production Vegetable Cropping Plan The following table outlines vegetables suitable for multiple cropping cycles per year in the Indian subcontinent (Pakistan and India), aligned with PQNK - Natural Ecosystem Science for Production Agriculture. It includes short-duration crops, their suitable seasons, number of possible cycles per year, and whether ethanol treatment is required for temperature extremes (above 35°C or below 10°C). Notes: • Seasons: • Spring: February–April (four-season areas). • Summer: May–June (tropical areas, pre-monsoon). • Monsoon: June–September (tropical and four-season areas). • Fall: October–November (four-season areas). • Winter: December–February (cool in four-season areas, cold in northern regions). • Cycles/Year: Short-duration crops enable 2–4 harvests per year with crop rotation and bed-furrow irrigation, aligning with PQNK’s continuous production goal. • Bed-Furrow Irrigation: Used exclusively to maintain soil moisture and temperature, delivering water efficiently to crop roots while preventing waterlogging. • Soil Management: Nutrients are available in mineral form, optimized by microbial activity. Crop rotation and organic matter (e.g., crop residues, compost) maintain soil health without legume intercropping. • Ethanol Treatment: Applied to crops sensitive to temperatures above 35°C (e.g., lettuce, spinach, coriander in tropical summers) or below 10°C (e.g., radish, carrot, turnip in cold winters) to enhance stress tolerance. • Regional Considerations: • Tropical Regions (e.g., Sindh, Tamil Nadu): Focus on heat-tolerant crops like okra; ethanol for cool-season crops in winter. • Four-Season Areas (e.g., Punjab, Uttar Pradesh): Rotate cool- and warm-season crops; ethanol for temperature-sensitive crops during extremes. • Cold Regions (e.g., Himachal Pradesh, Kashmir): Use protected cultivation (e.g., poly-houses, trenches) for winter crops; ethanol for cold-sensitive crops. • PQNK Alignment: Rotate crops from different families (e.g., Brassicaceae: radish, turnip, cabbage; Apiaceae: carrot, coriander; Solanaceae: potato) to prevent pest/disease buildup. Bed-furrow irrigation and microbial-driven nutrient availability ensure year-round soil coverage and productivity. • Sources: Adapted from regional agricultural practices and vegetable production data for the Indian subcontinent. •
