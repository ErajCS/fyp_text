farming:

decline.

10 inches below soil).

How PQNK Works
How PQNK Works:
• Breaking hardpan.
• Raised beds (42 inches wide, 8–10 inches high) with
furrows.
Janter cover crop and mulch for soil health.
Transplant nursery plants in the middle of the beds at 4-
foot intervals.
Cut mother shoots at 1-2 feet to stimulate tillering and
have unformed sugarcane stalks. However, if multiple
harvests are planned, mother shoots shouldn't be cut.
Lateral irrigation (up to 90% water savings).
• Selective harvesting of mature stalks at soil level for
multiple harvests per year, if so desired.
• Mulch trash to support microbes.
PQNK uses raised beds and furrows to keep soil healthy.
Janter cover crops and mulch maintain moderate soil
temperatures and restrict water evaporation and also feed
microbes. We transplant nursery plants, cut mother shoots to
boost tillering for one crop per year, and use water-saving
lateral irrigation feeding through photos by siphon tubes. Like
bamboo, we may selectively harvest mature stalks at soil level
multiple times a year, mulching trash to sustain growth.

Ecological Mechanisms
Ecological Mechanisms
Nature's Provision: Microbes (Azotobacter,
Trichoderma) convert minerals to nutrients.
Nature's Protection: BT-like organisms,
Pseudomonas fluorescens control pests/diseases.
• Healthy plants resist recycling (pests/diseases
target weak plants).
• No red rot appears even after many harvests.
PQNK taps nature's genius. Microbes like
Azotobacter and Trichoderma convert minerals
and cycle nutrients, feeding plants without
fertilizers. BT-like organisms and Pseudomonas
control pests and diseases, keeping sugarcane
strong. Healthy plants resist nature's recycling
system, which targets only weak plants. PQNK
plantations had no red rot across harvests!
