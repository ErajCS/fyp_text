Profile of Founder Chairman Mr. Asif Sharif
A Trailblazing Innovator:
Mr. Asif Sharif: A Visionary Agricultural Pioneer, A Trailblazing Innovator, Philanthropist,
and Global Citizen: Mr. Asif Sharif is a visionary and innovative agricultural pioneer,
entrepreneur, engineer, inventor, and philanthropist. Born into the esteemed SirGroh family of
Faisalabad, renowned for its contributions to education, agriculture, and community service, Mr.
Sharif has upheld the family's legacy and virtues. His expertise encompasses marketing, project
development in agriculture, production process development & mechanization, forward
engineering, alternate energies, value addition and responsible citizenship.
Mr. Sharif is a trailblazing innovator who has revolutionized the agricultural industry with his numerous inventions and contributions. He
designed, developed, modified, altered, adopted and introduced the a multitude of agricultural machinery, including soil scrapers, fine
levelers, ridgers, border discs, ditchers, reversible disc ploughs, plate planters, tractor mounted sprayers, hoeing machines, sugarcane
planters, stubble shavers, sugarcane harvesters, combine harvesters, cotton pickers, peanut harvesters, hay & forage machines, bailers,
potato planting and harvesting machines, grain silos, and wide-span warehousing. His ingenuity extends to designing, developing, and
manufacturing machinery for land leveling, customized precision laser leveling, raised-bed cropping, precision seeding, compost banding,
fertilizer side dressing, transplanting, weeding, and soil aerating. He introduced various species of plants, improved seeds, hybrids,
especially of Corn, Cotton, Sunflower.
Groundbreaking Initiatives
Mr. Sharif's groundbreaking initiatives have transformed agricultural practices and significantly improved crop yields. He developed a
method for growing rice on raised beds in moist soil, achieving a remarkable 70% reduction in water usage. Additionally, he spearheaded
the "One Acre Prosperity" (OAP) process, empowering smallholders to achieve financial independence.
PQNK: A Sustainable Natural Farming System
Mr. Sharif challenged the conventional/industrial tillage based crop production process, which has been used for millennia. He developed a
new production system called PQNK (Paedar Qudratti Nizam Kashatqari), which means sustainable natural farming system. This system
does not require tillage or agrochemicals and reduces water use by over 70%. It also produces food at the lowest cost and highest quality
ever, surpassing even organic standards.

Profile of Founder Chairman Mr. Asif Sharif
Contributions to Business and Agriculture:
Beyond his technical expertise, Mr. Sharif has made significant contributions to the business and agricultural sectors. He served as President
of the Pakistan Belgium Business Development Forum, Honorary Consul for the Republic of Poland, and a member of the Board of
Directors of Zarai Taraqiati Bank (Agri. Dev. Bank). Additionally, he actively participated in the advisory boards of various governmental
and private sector bodies.
Mr. Sharif played a pivotal role in introducing and establishing renowned global companies in Pakistan, including Ford Motor Company,
Sperry New Holland, Ford New Holland, Case IH, Toft, Long Manufacturing, Butler, Howard, Ursus, and Kenwood to name few.
A Global Citizen:
Mr. Sharif is a global citizen who has undergone training in the United States, Australia, and Europe and has traveled extensively across five
continents for study, training, business, and leisure. His expertise has led to the development of major projects and the presentation of
technical papers on crop production processes and methods. He has transformed numerous farms by implementing sound programs for
mechanization, management, operations, farm design, irrigation, and production systems, knowledge-based skills development, and timely
provision of resources for enhanced agricultural productivity.
Mr. Asif Sharif is an exemplary individual who has dedicated his life to improving agriculture and empowering communities. His
contributions have had a profound impact on the lives of millions of people around the world. He is an inspiration to us all.
A Beloved Son, Husband, Father, and Grandfather:
Beyond his professional achievements, Mr. Sharif is also a beloved husband, father, and grandfather. He is a pillar of strength for his family,
providing unconditional love and support. His grandchildren, in particular, hold a special place in his heart, and he cherishes the opportunity
to spend time with them and teach them about the importance of agriculture.
A Trustworthy and Compassionate Leader:
Mr. Sharif is a trustworthy and compassionate leader, earning the respect of his colleagues and the admiration of the communities he serves.
He is known for his integrity, his commitment to social responsibility, and his dedication to making a positive impact on the world.
A Philanthropist with a Heart for All lifeforms:
He is particularly passionate about animal welfare, life in the soil - all lifeforms.
