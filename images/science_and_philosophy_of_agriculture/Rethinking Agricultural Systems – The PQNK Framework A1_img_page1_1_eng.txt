The image titled "Rethinking Agricultural Systems: The PQNK Framework | From Extraction to Ecosystem" is a visual guide that contrasts two agricultural models: the traditional Broken Linear System and the more sustainable Closed-Loop Ecosystem, illustrated through the framework of PQNK (Paedar Quadratti Nizam Kashatqari).

### Description:

1. **Left Side: The Broken Linear System**
   - This section depicts the "Crisis - The Anti-Ecosystem Model." 
   - It highlights "The Four Fractures":
     - **Fractured Structure**: Showcases issues like hardpan and erosion.
     - **Fractured Cycle**: Indicates that chemistry is replacing biology in agricultural practices.
     - **Fractured Hydrology**: Illustrates problems such as inundation and aquifer depletion.
     - **Fractured Immunity**: Depicts reliance on monoculture and pesticides.
   - The central iconography includes a tractor representing tillage, external inputs like fertilizers, and a fragile crop, indicating the vulnerabilities of this system.

2. **Center: The Paradigm Shift**
   - This area emphasizes a transition from the first model to the second, showcasing a circular flow that indicates a movement toward sustainability.

3. **Right Side: The Closed-Loop Ecosystem**
   - Depicts the framework of a healthy agricultural model, which is part of the PQNK philosophy. 
   - It emphasizes five key steps:
     1. **Plant without Disturbance**: Focuses on minimal soil disruption.
     2. **Correct Chemistry**: Ensures the right chemical balance in the soil.
     3. **Build Structure**: Utilizes mulch to promote healthy growth.
     4. **Biomass Recycled In Situ**: Stresses recycling nutrients within the ecosystem.
     5. **Harvest Nutrient-Dense Food**: Highlights the end goal of producing high-quality food.
   - Accompanying the steps are visual symbols representing healthy soil ecosystems, nutrients, and the overall health of the ecosystem.

4. **Axioms**
   - Four axioms on the right side summarize foundational principles of the PQNK framework, focusing on the importance of soil health and water replenishment.

### Purpose:
The image aims to educate viewers on the detrimental impacts of traditional agricultural practices while promoting a regenerative framework that views farmers as ecosystem orchestrators. It advocates for sustainable practices that prioritize soil health and ecological balance, ultimately leading to enhanced food quality and ecological resilience.
