     - **Problems**: 
       - Low porosity (less than 10% air).
       - Poor microbial activity.
       - Low water retention.
     - **Solutions**:

       - Root exudates that feed beneficial fungi and bacteria.
     - **Benefits**: 
       - Hydraulic conductivity increases by 50-300%.

       - Water-holding capacity improved substantially.

   - **Sandy Soil**:
     - **Problems**: 
       - Nutrient leaching.
       - Low water retention.
     - **Solutions**: 
       - Fibrous roots from crops like clover and rye to form a 'mesh.'
       - Mycorrhizae to bind sand particles.
     - **Benefits**: 
       - Increased water-holding capacity.
       - Enhanced nutrient retention, with improvements in crop-available nutrients.
