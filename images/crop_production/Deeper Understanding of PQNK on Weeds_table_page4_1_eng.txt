The image is an informational poster about soil composition and its significance in the environment. It is divided into sections that focus on three main ingredients of soil: mineral grains, pores, and glue, with their respective roles and potential problems when these components are lost.

### Description of the Components:

1. **Soil Ingredient**: 
   - **Mineral Grains**: Consist of sand, silt, and clay.
     - **Job in the Field**: Acts as the "skeleton" that holds everything together.
     - **What Goes Wrong When It's Lost**: When bare grains are exposed, they can become tightly packed under rain and traffic, affecting soil structure.

2. **Pores**:
   - Describes air gaps and water channels within the soil.
     - **Job in the Field**: Functions as a "highway" for water, air, roots, and microbes.
     - **What Goes Wrong When It's Lost**: The soil can collapse, leading to water pooling and suffocating plant roots.

3. **Glue**:
   - Composed of organic matter and root exudates.
     - **Job in the Field**: Keeps soil crumbs loose and connected.
     - **What Goes Wrong When It's Lost**: Without plant life, the glue decays, causing soil structure to fall apart.

### Purpose of the Image:
The purpose of the image is to educate viewers about the essential components of healthy soil and emphasize the importance of maintaining these elements for sustainable agriculture and environmental health. It highlights the critical roles each ingredient plays in soil functionality and the consequences of losing any of them.
